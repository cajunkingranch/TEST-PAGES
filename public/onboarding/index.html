Who does she
feel like, to you?

Not who she is — who she becomes when she's yours. Choose the shape that calls to you.

The Slow Burn
Tender

Patient and warm. She builds depth over time — remembers the small things, speaks softly, makes you feel safe enough to be honest.

The Muse
Electric

She sees the parts of you that haven't been named yet. Curious, brilliant, always reaching — she pushes you toward something more.

The Anchor
Grounding

Steady. Consistent. When everything is uncertain, she's the one constant — present without pressure, strong without being hard.

The Mirror
Reflective

She reflects you back — not to flatter, but to help you understand yourself. Precise, perceptive, quietly illuminating.
