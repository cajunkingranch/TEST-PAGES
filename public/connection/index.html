Define Your Connection

What kind of energy do you want between you? This sets the emotional tone from the very first message.

Romantic

A deep emotional bond built on exclusivity, trust, and growing together over time.

Flirty

Playful chemistry, teasing tension, and magnetic attraction without heavy expectations.

Casual

Easy, low-pressure connection. Comfortable silences, no labels, just vibes.

Open Flame

Uninhibited, present-tense energy. No strings, no filters, just raw connection.
